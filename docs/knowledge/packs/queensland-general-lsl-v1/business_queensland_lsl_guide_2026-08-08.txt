Publisher: Queensland Government, Business Queensland
Source: Long service leave entitlements and continuous service
Reviewed: 8 August 2026

The guide explains that Queensland long service leave information is governed by the Industrial Relations Act 2016 where the federal system does not provide an entitlement. It describes 8.6667 weeks after 10 years continuous service, a further 4.3333 weeks after another 5 years, possible pro-rata payment on termination after 7 years subject to conditions, and the importance of considering continuous service and the employee's circumstances. It states that the ordinary or higher applicable rate is relevant to payment and gives a formula for casual or regular part-time hours.

This short extract is used for plain-language explanation and limits. It is not a service-history record, calculator input, or legal advice.
