Sections 93, 94, 95, 97, 98, 103 and 105 are retained as the reviewed legal source for this pack. Section 93 defines continuous service for this division as service with the same employer, wholly in Queensland or partly in and partly outside Queensland. Section 95 provides, for an employee other than a seasonal employee, 8.6667 weeks of long service leave after 10 years continuous service and proportionate payment on termination after at least 7 years, subject to the stated conditions before 10 years. Section 97 addresses taking leave, section 98 addresses the payment rate, and sections 103 and 105 address casual or regular part-time continuity and payment.

This is a short curated extract, not a bulk reproduction of the Act. The Act remains the authoritative source for the full text, exceptions, definitions, transitional provisions and application rules.
