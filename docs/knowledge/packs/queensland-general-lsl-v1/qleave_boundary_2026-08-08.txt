Publisher: Queensland Government, Business Queensland
Source: QLeave – Portable long service leave in Queensland
Reviewed: 8 August 2026

The Queensland Government identifies QLeave as a portable long service leave scheme for eligible workers in specified industries. This pack retains that identification only to make a scope boundary explicit. It excludes QLeave registration, levy, return, claim, reimbursement, payment and other operational procedure content. No QLeave operational answer may be derived from this pack.
